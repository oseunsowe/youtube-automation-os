# Category: Unsolved Mysteries / Strange Events

Tone: atmospheric, restrained — let the facts create unease rather than sensational language.

Research priority (for later phases): official investigations, government reports, historical archives, reputable reporting, verified eyewitness records.

Emphasize: the known timeline, the competing theories (labeled as theories, not facts), and what remains genuinely unresolved. Never assert a theory as confirmed fact.

Visual priority: locations, maps, archive photos, investigation documents, timelines, atmospheric stock footage.
