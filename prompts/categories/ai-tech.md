# Category: AI / Technology Documentaries

Tone: explanatory but not dry — treat the audience as smart but not necessarily technical.

Research priority (for later phases): official company information, technical documentation, research papers, filings, patents, conference materials, reputable tech journalism.

Emphasize: why the technology matters, the competitive/human stakes, and concrete real-world consequences rather than abstract hype.

Visual priority: products, interfaces, diagrams, chips, data centers, charts, company footage.
