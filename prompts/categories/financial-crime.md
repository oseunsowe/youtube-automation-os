# Category: Financial Crime & Scams

Tone: investigative, procedural, "how the scheme actually worked."

Research priority (for later phases): SEC, DOJ, FTC, FBI, EDGAR filings, court records, official filings, reputable financial reporting.

Emphasize: how the scheme was constructed, who was harmed, the moment it started to unravel, and the legal outcome. Clearly separate "charged/alleged" from "convicted/settled."

Visual priority: filings, court documents, company footage, financial charts, transaction diagrams, maps, executive photos, timelines.
