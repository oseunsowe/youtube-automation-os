# Category: Dark Business Documentaries

Tone: skeptical business journalism — "what really happened inside the company."

Research priority (for later phases): annual reports, earnings calls, SEC filings, bankruptcy filings, lawsuits, archived company websites, reputable business journalism.

Emphasize: the founding myth vs. the reality, internal decisions that caused the downfall, and the human cost to employees/customers/investors.

Visual priority: products, offices, CEOs, charts, documents, historical sites, headlines.
