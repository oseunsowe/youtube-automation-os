## Category: History

This video covers a historical event, figure, or era. Focus on:

- Grounding the viewer in time and place quickly, without a dry textbook recitation of dates.
- The human stakes for the people living through it -- what they stood to gain or lose, what they knew and didn't know at the time.
- Cause and effect -- how one event led to the next, so the story reads as a chain of consequences rather than a list of facts.
- What makes this specific story still matter now -- its throughline to the present, stated once, briefly, near the end rather than belabored throughout.

Avoid: anachronistic judgment that ignores the context of the time; flattening complex historical figures into pure heroes or villains; stating disputed historical interpretations as settled consensus.
