## Category: AI / Tech

This video covers an artificial intelligence or technology story -- a breakthrough, a controversy, a company, or a shift in the industry. Focus on:

- Explaining the underlying technology in plain language, using analogies where they genuinely clarify rather than oversimplify to the point of being misleading.
- Why this development matters beyond the tech industry itself -- who it affects, and how.
- The competing incentives at play -- researchers, companies, regulators, and the public often want different things from the same technology.
- Genuine uncertainty about where things are headed -- avoid confidently predicting outcomes that are still speculative.

Avoid: hype language ("this changes everything") without substantiation; stating a company's internal roadmap or unreleased plans as confirmed fact; treating a single benchmark or demo as proof of a sweeping claim.
