## Category: Financial Crime

This video covers a fraud, scam, embezzlement, market manipulation, or other financial crime. Focus on:

- How the scheme actually worked, explained clearly enough that a non-finance viewer can follow the mechanics (what money moved, from whom, to whom, and how the deception was maintained).
- The human cost -- who lost money, savings, or trust, not just the aggregate dollar figure.
- The specific decisions and red flags along the way -- where it could have been caught, and why it wasn't.
- How it was eventually uncovered, and what happened to the people responsible.

Avoid: turning the perpetrator into an admirable antihero; giving so much technical detail that the story stops moving; presenting unverified rumors as settled fact.
