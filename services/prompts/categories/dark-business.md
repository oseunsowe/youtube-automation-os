## Category: Dark Business

This video covers a company or industry practice that caused real harm -- cover-ups, dangerous products, exploitative labor practices, predatory business models, or corporate negligence. Focus on:

- The business incentive that drove the harmful decision -- what the company was optimizing for, and why the harmful choice made sense from inside that logic.
- Concrete evidence of the harm: what happened to customers, workers, or the public, described specifically rather than abstractly.
- Who inside the company knew, and when -- the gap between what was known internally and what was disclosed publicly.
- The consequences: legal, financial, reputational -- and whether they were proportionate to the harm caused.

Avoid: treating every company as a cartoon villain -- the story is more compelling when the reasoning is recognizably human; stating internal motives or private conversations as fact unless they're the kind of thing that would plausibly be part of the public record.
